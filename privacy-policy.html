Privacy Policy for ExamPrep

How ExamPrep collects, uses, stores, and protects your information.

App Name: ExamPrep Package ID: com.examprep.apply Effective Date: November 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ExamPrep (Android Package ID: com.examprep.apply). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

This Privacy Policy explains what information ExamPrep collects when you use our mobile application, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, and how you can access or delete your data.

Summary: ExamPrep uses Google Sign-In strictly to authenticate your account and sync your mock test scores, sectional progress, and theme preferences across your devices. We do not sell your personal data, do not display third-party behavioral ads, and do not access sensitive device permissions such as camera, location, contacts, microphone, or external storage.

2. Information We Collect

ExamPrep collects only the minimum information required to provide personalized exam preparation and cloud progress synchronization:

Data Category Specific Data Collected Purpose Storage Location
Account & Identity Google Account display name, email address, and unique Firebase User ID (UID) obtained via Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager) User authentication, greeting display, and isolating your private study records Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore (users/{uid})
Exam & Study Activity Mock test & PYQ attempt history (set ID, set title, category, total questions, correct/incorrect/skipped counts, time taken, timestamps) and sectional question progress Tracking accuracy, showing performance statistics, and syncing progress across devices Cloud Firestore (users/{uid}/exam_attempts and users/{uid}/set_progress)
App Preferences Selected display theme (Paper Mode or Dark Mode) Preserving your preferred reading appearance locally and across sessions On-device SharedPreferences & Cloud Firestore user profile

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect precise or approximate GPS location.
  • We do not access your contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, files, camera, or microphone.
  • We do not collect financial, payment, health, or government identification information.
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs or cross-app tracking identifiers.

3. Device Permissions

ExamPrep requests only the standard Android INTERNET permission, which is necessary to:

  • Authenticate you securely through Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication.
  • Synchronize your exam attempt history, progress, and theme settings with Cloud Firestore.

All bundled practice mock tests, sectional question sets, and previous-year question (PYQ) papers are packaged locally inside the application so they can be viewed quickly without downloading executable code.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Verify your identity and maintain your signed-in session.
  • Save and display your completed mock tests, accuracy metrics, and study history.
  • Remember your chosen UI theme (Paper Mode or Dark Mode).
  • Protect accounts against unauthorized access using strict per-user database security rules.

We never sell, rent, lease, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services & Data Processors

ExamPrep relies on trusted Google infrastructure services to provide authentication and cloud database functionality. These services process data under Google's security and privacy commitments:

  • Google Sign-In & Android Credential Manager: Used to authenticate your Google Account securely. (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore: Used to manage your authenticated session and store your user profile, exam attempts, and study progress. (Firebase Privacy & Security Information)

These infrastructure providers may process technical metadata (such as IP address or service request logs) strictly as needed to deliver reliable and secure authentication and database operations.

6. Data Security & Access Controls

We implement technical and organizational safeguards to protect your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the ExamPrep app and Google Firebase servers is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Data stored in Google Cloud Firestore is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Zero-Trust Per-User Isolation: Cloud Firestore security rules enforce strict ownership checks so only your authenticated account (request.auth.uid == userId) can read or write your profile, exam attempts, and progress records.

While we follow industry-standard security practices, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% infallible.

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your profile, exam attempt history, and study progress for as long as your account remains active so you can review your preparation history at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to request permanent deletion of your ExamPrep account and all associated Cloud Firestore data (including your user profile, exam attempts, and set progress records) at any time:

  1. Send an email to lizasahu4061@gmail.com from the Google email address associated with your ExamPrep account.
  2. Use the subject line: "ExamPrep Account & Data Deletion Request".
  3. Once verified, we will permanently delete your Firebase Authentication record and all Firestore documents under your user ID within 30 days, and send you a confirmation email.

To clear locally cached theme preferences immediately on your device, you may sign out from the Profile screen and clear app storage or uninstall ExamPrep from Android Settings.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe a child has provided personal information to ExamPrep without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete that data.
